# Product

## Register

brand + product

## Users

University students who want to study more effectively. They have lecture materials (PDFs, PowerPoint slides) and need to transform them into active recall practice. Their context: self-directed study sessions, exam prep, quiz-based learning at their own pace.

## Product Purpose

Lectiq turns uploaded lecture materials into AI-extracted topics and generates custom quiz questions (MCQ, free response, or both) with AI-graded feedback. It helps students study smarter by making active recall frictionless — upload a lecture file and get a practice quiz in minutes.

## Brand Personality

**Academic · Clean · Modern**

Voice: confident but approachable. Knowledgeable like a tutor, not dry like a textbook. Direct, no marketing fluff.

## Anti-references

Avoid the generic SaaS template at all costs. No cookie-cutter hero sections with purple-blue gradients, no identical card grids with icon + heading + body, no "streamline / empower / supercharge" language. This should not look like a boilerplate startup landing page.

## Design Principles

1. **Academic authenticity** — looks and feels like a serious study tool. Scholarly but not stuffy. Think well-designed course materials, not gamified quiz app.
2. **Clean clarity** — every element earns its place. Minimal decoration, maximum signal. Spacing and typography do the work.
3. **Confident restraint** — use color and motion deliberately, not decoratively. The design fades into the background so the content leads.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Sufficient contrast on all text. Reduced motion support. Keyboard-navigable interactive elements.
