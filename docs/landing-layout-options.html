Landing page: layout options

Four ways to restructure the page. The tiles below are captured at their real display size, so the reasoning text is legible in every option. That was the flaw in what is live: the asset was photographed at 1200px wide and rendered into a 468px column.

Measured on the live page. The hero image renders 468px wide from a 1200px source, a scale of 0.39. The 13px body text in the screenshot lands at about 5px on desktop and under 4px on a phone. It reads as texture, not text. Fixing that is what these options share.
Option 1

Stack the hero

Recommended

Image below the copy instead of beside it. The tile gets roughly 1000px rather than 468px, so the reasoning renders near its captured size and reads without touching the components. Copy sits above the fold; the image starts below it.

Option 2

Keep two columns, re-capture for the slot

Layout unchanged, so the page keeps its density and the CTA stays in the fold. The asset is captured at 468px wide with larger type, which means the components need a deliberate scale rather than being photographed and shrunk.

Cost: the screenshot stops being a faithful capture of the product at its real size. A 20px heading in the tile is not 20px in the app.
